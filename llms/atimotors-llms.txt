# Ati Robotics

> Ati Robotics builds autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) and AI orchestration software for enterprise factories. The Sherpa AMR family handles indoor and outdoor material movement at scale; Ati Fleet Manager and Ati Copilot coordinate fleets across the shop floor. Trusted by 70+ factories across 6 countries including manufacturers like Airbus, Hyundai, Daimler, and Bosch.

## Products (Sherpa AMR Family)

- [Sherpa 10K](https://www.atirobotics.ai/products/trolley/sherpa-10k/): Highest-payload trolley AMR, engineered for demanding factory towing applications up to 10,000 lbs (4,600 kg).
- [Sherpa XT Lite](https://www.atirobotics.ai/products/trolley/sherpa-xt-lite/): Rugged autonomous trolley tugger built for full-shift indoor and outdoor operation, up to 3,300 lbs (1,500 kg) payload.
- [Sherpa Pallet Mover](https://www.atirobotics.ai/products/pallet/sherpa-pallet-mover/): Autonomous pallet transport up to 3,300 lbs (1,500 kg) with LiDAR, cameras, and dynamic obstacle avoidance.
- [Sherpa Flexfork](https://www.atirobotics.ai/products/pallet/sherpa-flexfork/): Versatile AMR that autonomously lifts and transports trolleys up to 1,100 lbs (500 kg) using a single robust fork.
- [Sherpa Lifter 500](https://www.atirobotics.ai/products/flexible/sherpa-lifter-500/): Compact, customizable AMR for automated pallet handling and bin movement, 1,100 lbs (500 kg) lift capacity.
- [Sherpa Pivot](https://www.atirobotics.ai/products/flexible/sherpa-pivot/): Versatile autonomous material mover with modular mono fork and trailing unit attachments, up to 1,100 lbs (500 kg).
- [Sherpa Mecha](https://www.atirobotics.ai/products/sherpa-mecha/): Industrial humanoid-inspired robot built for unstructured manufacturing work.

## Solutions

- [Tugging](https://www.atirobotics.ai/solutions/tugging/): High-payload autonomous towing for demanding factory environments. Built around Sherpa 10K and Sherpa XT Lite.
- [Pallet Movement](https://www.atirobotics.ai/solutions/pallet-movement/): Autonomous pallet transport that eliminates forklift dependency. Sherpa Pallet Mover and Flexfork.
- [Lifting & Handling](https://www.atirobotics.ai/solutions/lifting-and-handling/): Autonomous bin, rack, and mixed-load handling for complex flows. Sherpa Mecha and Lifter Series.
- [Software & Orchestration](https://www.atirobotics.ai/solutions/orchestration/): Fleet Manager, Material Orchestration, and Copilot — the intelligent coordination layer for AMR fleets.

## Software

- [Ati Fleet Manager](https://www.atirobotics.ai/software/ati-fm/): VDA 5050 compliant multi-vendor fleet management platform with real-time traffic management and ERP/MES integration.
- [Ati Copilot](https://www.atirobotics.ai/software/ati-copilot/): Conversational AI interface for Ati Fleet Manager. Configure routes, resolve alerts, and pull reports in plain language.
- [Sanjaya](https://www.atirobotics.ai/software/sanjaya/): Real-time fleet insights, KPI reporting, predictive maintenance, and route analytics.
